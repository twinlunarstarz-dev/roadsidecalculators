Vehicle lockout

Car lockout cost calculator

Estimate the cost of a roadside unlock using the service-call price and optional after-hours or difficult-entry charges. If a locksmith or roadside provider gives you a dispatch price, enter it for a more specific estimate.

Before paying out of pocket

  • Check roadside assistance through your auto insurer, manufacturer, credit card or auto club.
  • Ask for the full dispatch price and any after-hours fee before the technician is sent.
  • Confirm whether the service is a simple unlock or involves key replacement/programming.

Emergency situations

If a child, vulnerable person or pet is locked in a dangerously hot or cold vehicle, or there is another immediate threat to life or safety, contact emergency services instead of delaying for price comparison.
