Lockout

Vehicle lockout cost calculator

Lockout prices depend more on provider, dispatch distance, time and entry complexity than on “metro size,” so the calculator now models those factors directly.

Before paying out of pocket

  • Check roadside assistance through your insurer, vehicle manufacturer, credit card or auto club.
  • Ask for the total dispatch price before the technician is sent.
  • If a child, vulnerable person or pet is locked in a hot or dangerous vehicle, treat it as an emergency and contact emergency services rather than price-shopping.
