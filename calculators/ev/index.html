EV charging

EV charging cost calculator

Estimate the cost of charging an electric vehicle at home or a public charger. Enter the battery capacity, starting and target charge, price per kWh and charging efficiency to estimate the energy drawn and session cost.

Charging losses vary with the vehicle, charger, power level and temperature.
Use the vehicle label or a recent driving average when available.

Charging-session estimate

The session calculation uses the change in battery state of charge to estimate energy added, then accounts for charging losses to estimate energy drawn from the grid. Public stations may also add session, parking, idle, tax or membership fees.

Cost per 100 miles

Vehicle energy use is commonly expressed as kWh per 100 miles. Multiplying that figure by your electricity price gives a simple driving-energy cost estimate. Weather, speed, HVAC use, towing and charging losses can change real-world cost.
