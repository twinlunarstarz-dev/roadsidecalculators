How rental reimbursement works

Rental reimbursement is typically an optional auto-policy benefit that helps pay for a temporary vehicle after a covered loss, subject to the policy's daily and duration limits. The declarations page or claim handler should provide the limits that apply to your claim.

Read the rental reimbursement guide →

What may remain out of pocket

A rental rate above the daily limit, uncovered days, upgraded vehicle classes, fuel, tolls, optional rental-company protection products and some fees may not be reimbursed. Coverage varies by policy and claim circumstances.
