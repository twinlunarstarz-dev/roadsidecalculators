Rental reimbursement calculator

Collision coverage and rental reimbursement are not the same thing. This calculator models the rental bill and then applies the separate daily/total reimbursement limits from your policy or an at-fault insurer.

Enter 0 if you have no rental reimbursement.
Use 0 if your policy has no separate dollar cap.

Coverage clarification

Rental reimbursement is optional coverage that pays rental expenses after a covered loss, subject to policy limits. Collision/comprehensive coverage pays for covered damage to your own car and typically has a deductible; it does not automatically mean your rental is free. Damage-waiver products sold by the rental company are a separate issue again.

Progressive describes typical rental reimbursement options in the range of about $40–$70 per day for 30 or 45 days, depending on policy/state, and Allstate likewise explains that daily and duration limits apply. Use the numbers from your own declarations page or claim handler.
