What is usually part of an installed tire price?

Depending on the retailer, mounting, balancing, valve or TPMS service, disposal and lifetime rotation or balancing may be bundled or billed separately. Taxes and road-hazard plans can also change the checkout total.

Tire safety comes first

Some tread-area punctures can be repaired when they meet tire-industry criteria. Sidewall or shoulder damage, large punctures, excessive wear or internal damage may require replacement. Have the tire inspected rather than choosing a repair based only on price.
