Safety note

A puncture can sometimes be repaired when it is in the repairable tread area and meets tire-industry criteria. Sidewall/shoulder damage, large punctures, run-flat damage after loss of pressure, or insufficient tread can require replacement. Have a tire professional inspect it rather than relying on price alone.

Retail installation bundles differ: mounting, balancing, TPMS service, disposal and lifetime services may be bundled or separate. Discount Tire, for example, describes installation as including mounting, balancing, valve/TPMS service and disposal-related services.
