Towing cost calculator

Uses a $50–$150 hook-up/base range and $2.50–$7.00 per mile as a broad non-member baseline, then applies vehicle and after-hours factors. Replace assumptions with a written local quote whenever possible.

Low estimate, dollars

What can change the price?

  • Winching, recovery, storage, tolls, parking access and specialty equipment are not included.
  • Membership or insurance roadside benefits can reduce your out-of-pocket amount substantially.
  • For EVs and AWD vehicles, follow the owner’s manual. A flatbed/platform is commonly preferred, but towing requirements are vehicle-specific.

Baseline checked against AAA guidance stating non-covered towing can involve a roughly $50–$150 base fee plus about $2.50–$7 per mile.
