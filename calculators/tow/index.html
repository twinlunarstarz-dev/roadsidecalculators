Towing

Towing cost calculator

Estimate a tow using the distance, vehicle class, hook-up fee and per-mile rate. The default range is a broad planning baseline; enter a local rate or written quote whenever you have one.

Enter the provider's dispatch or hook-up fee if known.

What affects towing cost?

  • Hook-up or dispatch: the base charge to send the truck and load the vehicle.
  • Distance: many providers add a per-mile rate, although local flat-rate towing is also common.
  • Vehicle and equipment: larger vehicles, recovery work or specialty equipment can raise the price.
  • Timing: nights, holidays and high-demand conditions may add a surcharge.

Costs not included here

Winching, off-road recovery, storage, impound charges, tolls, taxes, parking access and administrative fees can be separate. Ask for the destination and total expected charge before dispatch when possible.
