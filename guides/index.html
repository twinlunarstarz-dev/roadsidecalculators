Roadside guides

Understand the bill before you approve the service.

Learn what commonly drives towing, tire, repair, lockout, charging and rental costs—and what red flags deserve another question or a second opinion before money changes hands.

When you're stranded, urgency is not the same as certainty. Use these guides and calculators as independent reference points, not as instructions to blindly authorize service. When the situation is safe enough to do so, get the price and scope in writing, ask what evidence supports an expensive recommendation, compare another provider or opinion, and keep receipts and messages. If there is an immediate traffic, fire, medical, child or pet emergency, prioritize safety and emergency services over price comparison.
