Estimate disclaimer

Roadside Calculators provides planning tools and general educational information.

Calculator outputs are estimates, not quotes, guarantees, appraisals, insurance determinations, legal advice, mechanical diagnoses or instructions to authorize a particular service. Actual prices and coverage can vary by provider, jurisdiction, vehicle, market conditions, taxes, policy terms and the facts of a specific incident.

Whenever possible, replace default calculator assumptions with your own written quote, utility rate, vehicle specification, repair estimate or insurance policy limits. Verify towing and repair procedures in the vehicle manufacturer’s documentation.

In an emergency or dangerous roadside situation, prioritize personal safety and contact appropriate emergency services rather than relying on a calculator.
